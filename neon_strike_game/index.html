Mission protocol

1. Complete the objective.
Eliminate the required hostiles. Commander missions begin after the guard units are cleared.

2. Read enemy behavior.
Runners close quickly, gunners telegrah their shot with a thin laser before firing, tanks absorb damage, and bosses use multi-phase volleys.

3. Choose an upgrade.
After each cleared sector, select one permanent run upgrade before deploying to the next mission.

4. Keep moving.
Your dash briefly protects you from damage. Its cooldown refills automatically. Build EMP charge by eliminating enemies; press E at 100% to clear hostile shots and damage the entire arena.

Touchscreen controls

Use the virtual joystick on the left to move. Touch & drag the FIRE joystick on the right to aim freely in 360°, or tap to fire with Smart Auto-Aim. Toggle aim mode anytime with the AIM button. PHASE DASH evades damage briefly. Landscape orientation is recommended.

Field intel

Collect green integrity shards to restore suit health. Gold cores in Scrapline stay visible until collected, count toward the salvage objective, and restore up to 15 suit integrity. If all hostiles are cleared and a core is still missing, the sector reveals salvage caches. Magenta overdrive drops increase your fire rate, and blue shields absorb a hit. Upgrades stack for the current campaign. Your highest score and unlocked mission progress are stored on this device using browser local storage.
